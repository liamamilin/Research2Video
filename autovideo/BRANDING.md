# Reusable video branding

Branding is a production setting, independent of Matrix (audience/domain), Column
(recurring content product), and Production Recipe (visual presentation). One brand
can be shared by every domain and presentation recipe.

## Try the neutral Demo

In the research console, open **设置 → 视频品牌**. Choose **安装 Demo 示例**, preview the logo and listen to the cue, then choose
**保存并设为默认**. The installer preserves an existing Demo profile and assets.
It generates the example logo and four-note sound locally; no external music,
image service, account or download is required.

The same installation is available from the engine API:

```http
POST /api/brands/demo/install
PUT /api/brands/active
Content-Type: application/json

{"profile":"demo"}
```

Or run from `autovideo/`:

```sh
g0/.venv/bin/python scripts/install_demo_brand.py --activate
```

Fresh installations have no active brand. This feature supplies Demo Studio only;
personal names, marks, voice signatures and exclusive melodies can be added later.

## What a branded video contains

- Demo starts directly with the content and gently reveals its corner mark over
  0.6 seconds. An optional 0.5–2 second standalone brand opening remains available.
- A corner mark with logo, brand name and the packaging's column label.
- The existing content, source card and narration; final captions and chapters
  shift by the opening duration while the content originals keep their timeline.
- A 3–6 second tail. Optional generic CTA icons (点赞、收藏、关注) take its first
  1.5 seconds, followed by the logo/name/tagline card. Enabling CTA does not add
  time to the tail. The Demo default is 4 seconds total.
- An optional sonic cue starting 0.2 seconds after the brand card begins.
  Longer cues are trimmed and faded to the selected end-card duration. They do
  not play under the narration.

The brand stage runs after packaging and optional background music, and works
with hybrid cards, FFmpeg cards and Remotion outputs. It replaces the generic
intro, outro, corner badge and package sting for newly branded jobs. There is no
extra generic intro before the content. Source-card settings are retained.
Rendering currently uses the project's 1920×1080, 30fps output and macOS font setup.
The optional standalone opening cue fades out before the content starts. Set
`intro_seconds` to `0` for the recommended direct opening; set
`corner_reveal_seconds` to `0.6` for the gentle reveal (or `0` for an immediate
mark). Existing snapshots without the reveal/CTA options retain their prior
behavior. These options do not alter narration or caption timing.

`video.mp4` is the branded final. `unbranded.mp4` retains its immediate source;
`content.mp4` and content captions retain their existing meanings when packaging
is enabled. `brand-qa.json` records the frozen brand hash, frame counts, full
decode result, content/end-card durations and sonic start. Final chapters include
the optional brand opening, CTA and brand card; final captions include only the
standalone opening offset.

## Profiles and overrides

Profiles are local JSON records, managed with `PUT /api/brands/{id}`:

```json
{
  "name": "Demo Studio",
  "tagline": "把知识，变成画面。",
  "accent": "#28775b",
  "background": "#f5f6f2",
  "text": "#172b23",
  "intro_seconds": 0,
  "corner_reveal_seconds": 0.6,
  "outro_seconds": 4,
  "cta": {"enabled": true, "message": "喜欢这期内容，欢迎"}
}
```

The CTA uses three platform-neutral outlined icons, always ordered like, save,
follow. Its optional single-line message is limited to 24 characters; turn it off
with `cta.enabled:false`. The images and animation are generated by trusted code,
with no uploaded platform graphics or external requests. The sonic cue plays
after CTA; the action row is silent unless its short sound option is enabled.

Enable **正文沿用品牌配色** (`apply_to_content:true`) to carry the profile's
background, text and accent into new fast-card and hybrid-card productions,
including captions and flow connectors. The palette freezes in the production
plan; historical snapshots retain their saved appearance. Real screenshots and
semantic chart/entity colors keep their original meaning. The checkbox defaults
off for existing profiles and does not recolor other presentation families.
The corner mark displays a column subtitle only when a column label was selected;
it does not use the common packaging preset's placeholder as a brand name.
The brand end card contains the selected identity and tagline, without a fixed
engine or project signature.
Enable **角标显示品牌 Slogan** (`corner_tagline:true`) to also show the tagline
under the corner's brand name, in place of a column subtitle. The same text is
editable as **品牌 Slogan**. A long corner line is shortened to fit; the end card
retains its complete text. Omitted settings keep historical corner layouts.

New production plans reserve a shared top rail for content metadata and the
brand. Chapter/date information sits on the left, with matching top/baselines
and a gap before the identity on the right. Frozen plans without `brand_layout`
retain their prior layout. This covers information/explanation, fast/hybrid
cards and creative forms.

`cta.sound:true` adds three quiet synthesized plucks at 0.04, 0.16 and 0.28 s of
the CTA, synchronized with the icon entrances. Missing/false remains silent.
The uploaded brand melody starts after the CTA and does not overlap narration.

`GET /api/brands` lists profiles and the active selection. Use
`PUT /api/brands/active` with `{"enabled":false}` to turn the default off.
For one video, `/api/jobs` and `/api/production/resolve` accept:

```json
{"brand":{"enabled":true,"profile":"demo"}}
```

Use `{"brand":{"enabled":false}}` to disable branding for that video. When
omitted, new jobs inherit the active engine profile. Matrix, column and production
configuration can use the same `render.brand` setting, with the existing
production-default precedence. Changing the active brand affects new submissions.

Uploads use `POST /api/brands/{id}/assets/logo` or `/assets/sonic` with a JSON
`base64` field. Each upload is at most 4 MiB. Logos use PNG (16–2048 pixels),
including transparency. Sonic cues use 1–6 second, 16-bit PCM WAV, mono/stereo,
44.1/48 kHz. Uploaded paths, external URLs and executable templates are not accepted.
The corresponding GET serves the preview. Research-console proxies live at
`/api/video-brands` and require an administrator session and CSRF for mutations.

## Freeze and keep assets local

The engine stores profiles, hash-named assets and the active selection under
`g3/data/brands/`. Each job copies its chosen profile and assets into
`jobs/{id}/input/brand/`; `brand_resolved` and `production_plan` bind the snapshot
by SHA-256. Retry and revision use the saved inputs even after the current profile
changes or disappears. Historical jobs without a brand stay unbranded. An
idempotent replay returns its original job even when the active brand changes.

The open-source portion contains configuration contracts, uploads, snapshotting,
composition, the settings UI, tests and the neutral Demo generator. Runtime brand
profiles, uploaded logo/audio files, job inputs and rendered media stay in the
ignored data directory. Back up that directory separately. Avoid `git add -f` on
runtime assets. A future personal brand pack uses the same interface without
putting its identity or media into the public repository.
